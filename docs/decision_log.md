# SECTION 1 — DECISIONS
    These items represent the currently established project direction.

## ID       
    Decision                                                                                                    Status

## D-001    
    The game will be a high-fantasy medieval simulation.                                                        Approved

## D-002    
    Players will determine their own professions and lifestyles.                                                Approved

## D-003    
    The game will have one large, shared, persistent world.                                                     Approved

## D-004
    The world will continue operating while players are offline.                                                Approved

## D-005
    Player interaction will be central to the game experience.                                                  Approved

## D-006
    The initial game client will be map-based.                                                                  Approved

## D-007
    The long-term vision includes transitioning to a 3D world with avatars.                                     Approved

## D-008
    The browser game will use JavaScript.                                                                       Approved

## D-009
    The game will have a Node.js backend.                                                                       Approved

## D-010
    NodeBB will provide the forum.                                                                              Approved

## D-011
    Wiki.js will provide the wiki.                                                                              Approved

## D-012
    The game, forum, and wiki will share one player login experience.                                           Approved

## D-013
    Project work will be organized into Milestones, Epics, Issues, and Tasks.                                   Approved

## D-014
    Each project phase will be discussed before its development structure is finalized.                         Approved

## D-015
    The project will maintain one consolidated Master Project Log.                                              Approved

## D-016
    Phase 1 will prioritize planning, architecture, and documentation rather than coding.                       Approved

## D-017
    Phase 1 will contain three Epics: Core Game Design, Technical Architecture, and Development Standards.      Approved

## D-018
    Players must have meaningful opportunities for progression and major accomplishments regardless of 
    whether they pursue combat or non-combat gameplay. Neither path should be inherently superior in 
    overall opportunity for advancement.                                                                        Approved

## D-019
    Players will never be permanently locked into a particular profession or lifestyle. They may change
    activities and pursue different paths throughout the game.                                                  Approved

## D-020
    A character may develop multiple professions and participate in different activities. Players should
    not need separate characters to experience different gameplay paths.                                        Approved

## D-021
    Learned skills and knowledge will be retained when a player changes professions or activities.
    Changing lifestyles will not erase previously acquired knowledge or abilities.                              Approved

## D-022
    The world is shared across all players. Actions taken by one player can directly or indirectly affect 
    other players, regardless of whether those other players are online. Shared resources, such as ore 
    deposits, change in availability as players gather or consume them.                                         Approved

## D-023
    The game economy will maintain a balance between NPC-operated and player-operated businesses. NPC 
    businesses will help support the economy during periods of low player participation. As player-owned 
    businesses become more prevalent, NPC-operated businesses will decrease accordingly. NPCs will also 
    be available as employees to support player-owned businesses.                                               Approved

## D-024
    NPC employees may work a maximum of six hours per shift at a player-owned business. Shops operating 
    for longer periods must employ additional NPCs to cover the remaining hours.

    This limitation is intended to:
    - Prevent players from relying exclusively on NPC employees to operate businesses indefinitely without 
        meaningful operating costs.
    - Increase business upkeep costs for shops with extended operating hours.
    - Encourage owners to personally operate their businesses when they want to reduce expenses.
    - Make staffing and operating hours meaningful business-management decisions.
    Example: A shop operating 24 hours per day requires at least four NPC employees if the owner does 
    not work any shifts.                                                                                        Approved

## D-025
    NPCs will have independent lives within the game world. They will participate in the world's society 
    and follow their own activities and routines, helping create an authentic medieval simulation atmosphere. 
    NPCs are inhabitants of the world, not merely services or resources for players.                            Approved

## D-026
    For the initial game, NPCs will have established occupations, roles, and circumstances. They will follow 
    independent routines but will not independently advance through major life or career changes.               Approved

## D-027
    Players may pursue leadership roles within the game world, allowing them to become influential members 
    and leaders of societies and organizations. The initial scope of leadership will be established during 
    game design. The ability for players to hold the highest positions of authority is not part of the 
    initial commitment.                                                                                         Approved
