# SECTION 1 — DECISIONS
    These items represent the currently established project direction.

## ID           Status       
    Decision                                                                                                   

## D-001        Approved    
    The game will be a high-fantasy medieval simulation.                                                       

## D-002        Approved
    Players will determine their own professions and lifestyles.                                                

## D-003        Approved
    The game will have one large, shared, persistent world.                                                   

## D-004        Approved
    The world will continue operating while players are offline.                                               

## D-005        Approved
    Player interaction will be central to the game experience.                                                

## D-006        Approved
    The initial game client will be map-based.                                                              

## D-007        Approved
    The long-term vision includes transitioning to a 3D world with avatars.                                   

## D-008        Approved
    The browser game will use JavaScript.                                                                      

## D-009        Approved
    The game will have a Node.js backend.                                                                  

## D-010        Approved
    NodeBB will provide the forum.                                                                          

## D-011        Approved
    Wiki.js will provide the wiki.                                                                            

## D-012        Approved
    The game, forum, and wiki will share one player login experience.                                       

## D-013        Approved
    Project work will be organized into Milestones, Epics, Issues, and Tasks.                              

## D-014        Approved
    Each project phase will be discussed before its development structure is finalized.                      

## D-015        Approved
    The project will maintain one consolidated Master Project Log.                                            

## D-016        Approved
    Phase 1 will prioritize planning, architecture, and documentation rather than coding.                      

## D-017        Approved
    Phase 1 will contain three Epics: Core Game Design, Technical Architecture, and Development Standards.     

## D-018        Approved
    Players must have meaningful opportunities for progression and major accomplishments regardless of 
    whether they pursue combat or non-combat gameplay. Neither path should be inherently superior in 
    overall opportunity for advancement.                                                                  

## D-019        Approved
    Players will never be permanently locked into a particular profession or lifestyle. They may change
    activities and pursue different paths throughout the game.                                             

## D-020        Approved
    A character may develop multiple professions and participate in different activities. Players should
    not need separate characters to experience different gameplay paths.                                   

## D-021        Approved
    Learned skills and knowledge will be retained when a player changes professions or activities.
    Changing lifestyles will not erase previously acquired knowledge or abilities.                         

## D-022        Approved
    The world is shared across all players. Actions taken by one player can directly or indirectly affect 
    other players, regardless of whether those other players are online. Shared resources, such as ore 
    deposits, change in availability as players gather or consume them.                                      

## D-023        Dynamic Balance Between NPC and Player Business         Approved
    The game economy will maintain a balance between NPC-operated and player-operated businesses. NPC 
    businesses will help support the economy during periods of low player participation. As player-owned 
    businesses become more prevalent, NPC-operated businesses will decrease accordingly. NPCs will also 
    be available as employees to support player-owned businesses.                                        

## D-024        NPC Employee Working Limits                             Approved
    NPC employees may work a maximum of six hours per shift at a player-owned business. Shops operating 
    for longer periods must employ additional NPCs to cover the remaining hours.

    This limitation is intended to:
    - Prevent players from relying exclusively on NPC employees to operate businesses indefinitely without 
        meaningful operating costs.
    - Increase business upkeep costs for shops with extended operating hours.
    - Encourage owners to personally operate their businesses when they want to reduce expenses.
    - Make staffing and operating hours meaningful business-management decisions.
    Example: A shop operating 24 hours per day requires at least four NPC employees if the owner does 
    not work any shifts.                                                                                

## D-025        NPCs as Independent World Inhabitants                   Approved
    NPCs will have independent lives within the game world. They will participate in the world's society 
    and follow their own activities and routines, helping create an authentic medieval simulation atmosphere. 
    NPCs are inhabitants of the world, not merely services or resources for players.                         

## D-026        Stable NPC Circumstances                                Approved
    For the initial game, NPCs will have established occupations, roles, and circumstances. They will follow 
    independent routines but will not independently advance through major life or career changes.              

## D-027        Player LEadership and Governance                        Approved
    Players may pursue leadership roles within the game world, allowing them to become influential members 
    and leaders of societies and organizations. The initial scope of leadership will be established during 
    game design. The ability for players to hold the highest positions of authority is not part of the 
    initial commitment.                                                                                         

## D-028        Phased Player Experience and Presentation               Approved
    The initial game will use a relatively basic browser-based interface, with gameplay presented through 
    functional menus, maps, screens, and other interface elements. The initial client is not expected to 
    provide a fully immersive medieval simulation experience. The long-term vision is to progressively 
    develop the presentation and interaction systems, ultimately transitioning toward a 3D environment 
    with avatars and more immersive physical interactions.                                                      

## D-029        Initial Client Interaction Model                        Approved
    The initial browser-based client will provide an interactive map of the game world. Players will 
    primarily manage their characters, professions, businesses, activities, and other gameplay systems 
    through menus and management screens. The map will serve as a means of navigating and interacting 
    with the world, while menus and management screens will provide access to the majority of gameplay 
    functionality.  

## D-030        Race Based Starting Location Recommendations            Approved
    Players may choose their starting kingdom or region regardless of their character's race. However, 
    the game will recommend a starting location based on the character's race, reflecting that race's 
    historical homeland and cultural heritage. Players will not be restricted from starting elsewhere.

## D-031        Distinct Racial Heritage And Shared Regional Standards  Approved
    Each race will have a distinct cultural and historical background in addition to its physical 
    characteristics. Over time, interaction, migration, trade, and cultural exchange have resulted in 
    shared customs and standardized practices throughout different regions of the world. Racial heritage 
    will remain an important part of the world's identity, while regional societies will reflect the 
    integration of different races.

## D-032        Distinct Racial Abilities                               Approved
    Each playable race will have a collection of racial abilities that reflect its characteristics and 
    heritage. Most racial abilities will be associated with specific races, although some abilities 
    may be shared by multiple races.

## D-033        Passive Racial Abilities and Active Skill Abilities     Approved
    Racial abilities will primarily consist of passive advantages or characteristics inherent to a 
    character's race. Abilities acquired through skill development will primarily be active abilities 
    that players can use during gameplay. The specific abilities and their effects remain subject to 
    future design and balancing.

## D-034        No Race Based Profession Restrictions                   Approved
    All playable races will have access to all professions and skills. No profession or skill will 
    be restricted solely because of a character's race. Racial abilities may provide different passive 
    advantages, but every race must have meaningful opportunities to pursue any profession or gameplay path.

## D-035        Shared Racial Characteristics                           Approved
    All members of a particular race share that race's established racial traits and passive abilities. 
    Players cannot individually select or customize which inherent racial traits their character possesses.
    Individual characters will distinguish themselves through their chosen skills, professions, abilities, 
    accomplishments, and other forms of character development.

## D-036        Phased Introduction of Group Gameplay                   Approved
    The initial game release will emphasize primarily solo gameplay. Systems such as parties, guilds, 
    player organizations, mercenary companies, and other player-created groups are intended for introduction 
    during a future phase as the game transitions toward a more graphical and immersive experience. This does 
    not eliminate player interaction from the initial release. Players may still interact economically and 
    socially through the available browser-based systems.

## D-037        Player Driven Economy in Initial Release                Approved
    The initial release will include a player-driven economy. Players will be able to interact economically 
    through activities such as buying and selling goods, trading resources, and operating businesses. Formal 
    group gameplay, including parties, guilds, and player-created organizations, will be introduced in a later 
    development phase.

## D-038        Initial In Game Communication System                    Approved
    The initial release will include two player communication methods:
    1. Vicinity Chat: Allows players located in the same game area to communicate with one another.
    2. Private Messaging: Allows players to communicate privately and leave messages for other players, 
        including players who are currently offline. Messages will be available when the recipient returns.

## D-039        Public Player Profiles                                  Approved
    The initial release will include public player profiles that allow players to view information about other 
    characters and their accomplishments. The specific profile information and customization options will be 
    defined during a later design discussion.

## D-040        Character Progression System                            Approved
    The game will include a character progression system. The specific mechanics, including experience, levels, 
    skill advancement, profession progression, and how these systems interact, have not yet been determined.

## D-041        Expandable Game World                                   Approved
    The game world will support expansion as the player population grows. New territories may contain existing 
    civilizations, settlements, cultures, and potentially previously undiscovered races. Expansion may involve 
    discovering distant continents, crossing oceans, or exploring previously inaccessible regions. New areas will 
    not necessarily be untouched wilderness; they may already have established populations and societies.
