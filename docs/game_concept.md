# Arethtera
## Overall Game Concept
### Section 1: Game Overview
### Scope
    This section establishes the identity, purpose, and overall vision of Arethtera. It defines the experience the game intends to provide, the principles that guide its design, and the characteristics that distinguish it from traditional role-playing games.

### 1.1 Game Concept and Premise

    Arethtera is a persistent, high-fantasy medieval world in which players determine their own professions, lifestyles, ambitions, and place in society. Players are not required to become adventurers or participate in combat to experience meaningful progression or contribute to the world.
    
    The game supports a broad range of professions and ways of life. Players may pursue one profession or combine as many professions and career paths as they choose. Each profession will have its own progression tree, and advancement through profession-specific trees will contribute to a larger, overarching character progression tree.
    
    Arethtera distinguishes between four related aspects of player experience:
        - Professions: Occupations and career paths a character can pursue.
        - Activities: Individual tasks and actions available within the world.
        - Lifestyles: The ways players choose to live and participate in society.
        - Ambitions: Personal goals and achievements players work toward.
    
    These concepts are distinct but interconnected. A player's identity and experience are not limited to a single occupation or prescribed path.

### 1.2 Genre and Setting

    Arethtera is a high-fantasy life simulation MMORPG set in a persistent medieval-style world.
    
    The world will begin with an established NPC society, including existing settlements and civilizations. Players enter this world as participants in an existing society and can influence how it develops through their decisions and actions.
    
    NPCs will be active participants in the world rather than simply background characters, quest providers, or enemies. They will have meaningful roles in the functioning of society. The specific professions, responsibilities, routines, and systems governing NPC behavior will be defined in the relevant game-design sections.

### 1.3 Overall Vision

    Arethtera Studio's vision is to create a world where players can experience a way of life beyond the expectations of a traditional MMORPG. Players should be able to choose their own goals, develop their own capabilities, and make a place for themselves in the world.
    
    Progression and achievement will not be exclusive to adventuring or combat. Every supported progression pathway should provide meaningful opportunities for advancement, recognition, and accomplishment. A player should be able to reach the height of their chosen profession whether that path involves crafting, commerce, agriculture, hospitality, combat, or another supported pursuit.
    
    For example:
        - A master blacksmith should be able to achieve the highest levels of mastery and recognition in their profession, just as a legendary warrior can in theirs.
        - A merchant should be able to grow from an independent trader into the owner of a substantial commercial enterprise, potentially involving multiple shops, employees, suppliers, trade routes, and business locations.
        - A farmer should be able to develop a small farm into a major agricultural operation capable of supplying businesses, settlements, and potentially entire kingdoms.
        - An innkeeper should be able to build a small inn into a prominent destination that attracts travelers, merchants, adventurers, NPCs, and other players.
    
    These examples illustrate the intended breadth of opportunity. The detailed mechanics, requirements, and progression for each profession will be defined in their respective design sections.
    
    Players will also have influence over the shaping and development of the world. Their decisions and activities can contribute to the evolution of society. The extent and mechanics of that influence will be defined by the systems governing the world.

### 1.4 Core Design Philosophy

    Arethtera is designed around player choice, meaningful progression, and a world with its own rules and structure.
    
    There is no fixed limit on the number of professions a player may pursue. Players are free to develop multiple professions and combine career paths according to their interests and ambitions. No player is required to specialize in only one occupation or lifestyle.
    
    Every supported lifestyle should offer meaningful opportunities for advancement and achievement. Different professions may have different mechanics, rewards, and forms of recognition, but non-combat professions are not intended to be lesser or secondary paths. The world should depend on and recognize contributions from a variety of professions.
    
    Player freedom exists within the rules of the world. Players are expected to abide by the laws established by the kingdoms they inhabit. Breaking those laws can carry consequences, which may include punishment in a stockade, imprisonment in a jail, or a bounty being placed on the offender. The specific laws, enforcement systems, and consequences will be developed in the relevant design sections.
    
    Arethtera is also intended to grow and develop over time. The game will be designed to support future expansion through additional professions, territories, economic opportunities, activities, and world systems. This is a long-term direction; specific future features and release plans will be determined separately.

### 1.5 Game Difference

    The defining difference between Arethtera and many traditional RPGs and MMORPGs is that adventuring is an option, not a requirement.
    
    In many role-playing games, combat, exploration, and adventuring are central to character advancement. Arethtera is designed so that players can achieve meaningful progression without adopting that playstyle.
    
    The world does not revolve exclusively around adventurers. Non-combat professions are intended to be just as important to the functioning and development of society as combat-oriented professions. Farmers, merchants, blacksmiths, carpenters, innkeepers, warriors, guards, and other contributors all have meaningful roles within the world.
    
    Arethtera will also be a persistent world that continues to operate and evolve while individual players are offline. NPCs will continue participating in the world, and systems such as businesses, agriculture, markets, settlement development, and economic activity are intended to continue progressing independently of any one player's online presence. The precise simulation rules and mechanics will be defined in later design sections.
    
    The goal is not to remove adventuring from the MMORPG experience. It is to remove the requirement to adventure in order to progress, achieve, and participate meaningfully in the world.

### 1.6 Intended Experience

    Arethtera is intended to provide a high-fantasy life simulation experience in a world that includes magic, monsters, and other elements appropriate to its setting.
    
    Players may choose a quieter life focused on activities such as farming, crafting, trading, business ownership, or hospitality. Others may pursue adventuring, combat, exploration, and the challenges of the wider world. Players may also combine these paths rather than being confined to one style of play.
    
    The world will include systems that allow players to acquire land both within established settlements and outside settlement boundaries. A real estate system will govern land and property acquisition. A settlement management system will also be part of the game. The specific capabilities and rules of these systems—including how undeveloped land may be used and whether it can support the establishment of entirely new settlements—remain to be defined.
    
    Arethtera is intended to expand over time. Additional professions, territories, economic opportunities, activities, and world systems may be introduced through future updates. The professions and activities included in the initial release, as well as those reserved for later updates, have not yet been determined and will be established during further design work.
    
    The intended experience is a living fantasy world in which players can choose how they participate, pursue meaningful goals through different paths, and help shape the society around them. Arethtera does not prescribe one correct way to play: players create their own place in the world.
